# Color Palette

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 5**

---

## OVERVIEW

Schneider Electric's color palette consists of 6 primary colors and 4 secondary accent colors. This strategic selection ensures visual cohesion, brand recognition, and accessibility across all PowerPoint presentations.

---

## PRIMARY COLORS (6)

The primary palette drives the visual identity and should be used consistently across all presentations.

### 1. SE Life Green
- **Hex:** #3DCD58
- **RGB:** 61, 205, 88
- **Usage:** Primary brand color, used for large areas to create strong, consistent identity

### 2. SE Dark Green
- **Hex:** #0A2F24
- **RGB:** 10, 47, 36
- **Usage:** Depth, contrast, backgrounds, overlays, premium feel

### 3. SE Signature Green
- **Hex:** #009530
- **RGB:** 0, 149, 48
- **Usage:** Logo alternative, backgrounds, copy emphasis

### 4. SE Electric Green
- **Hex:** #5EFF59
- **RGB:** 94, 255, 89
- **Usage:** High-impact moments, highlights, calls to action (use sparingly)

### 5. SE Light Green
- **Hex:** #E7FFD9
- **RGB:** 231, 255, 217
- **Usage:** Soft supporting color, backgrounds, sections (keeps layouts fresh)

### 6. SE Light Gray
- **Hex:** #FAFAFA
- **RGB:** 250, 250, 250
- **Usage:** Neutral base, balance, secondary backgrounds

## SECONDARY COLORS (4)

Secondary colors complement the primary palette and should be used **selectively as accents** only — for charts, figures, sidebars, pull quotes, or data visualization.

### 1. SE Amber

### 3. SE Blue

### 4. SE Red
- **Hex:** #FF0050
- **RGB:** 255, 0, 80

---

## COLOR USAGE GUIDELINES

### ✅ DO

- Use primary greens consistently across presentations
- Build layouts around Life Green and Dark Green
- Use secondary colors strategically for data or emphasis
- Verify contrast ratios meet WCAG accessibility standards
- Test colors on actual slide backgrounds

### ❌ DON'T

- Use secondary colors for large text or backgrounds
- Mix all secondary colors in one design
- Use color alone to convey information (pair with patterns or labels)
- Substitute or create new brand colors
- Assume contrast without testing

---

## CONTRAST RATIOS

**Text contrast pairings:**
- Dark Green on Light Gray: 15.2:1 (AAA)
- Life Green on White: 5.8:1 (AA)
- Signature Green on White: 5.2:1 (AA)

All color combinations must meet minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body text.
