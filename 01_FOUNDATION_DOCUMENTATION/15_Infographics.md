# Schneider Electric Infographics

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 15**

---

## OVERVIEW

This document specifies infographic design and usage for Schneider Electric PowerPoint presentations. Infographics communicate complex data and concepts visually, improving comprehension and engagement.

**Core Principle:** Infographics simplify complexity, enable quick understanding, and make data memorable through visual design.

---

## INFOGRAPHIC DEFINITION

An infographic is a visual representation of information or data designed to:
- ✅ Simplify complex concepts
- ✅ Present data in visual/graphic form
- ✅ Improve comprehension and retention
- ✅ Guide viewer through information hierarchy
- ✅ Make information more engaging than text alone

**Infographics combine:**
- Visual design (shapes, colors, layout)
- Data or information (facts, statistics, concepts)
- Typography (labels, annotations, hierarchy)
- Icons or illustrations (visual support)

---

## INFOGRAPHIC TYPES

### Data Visualizations

**Purpose:** Display numerical data and statistics visually

**Common formats:**
- ✅ Charts (bar, line, pie, area)
- ✅ Graphs and plots
- ✅ Comparison matrices
- ✅ Progress or status indicators
- ✅ Heat maps or density visualizations

---

### Process Flows

**Purpose:** Show sequence, workflow, or progression

**Common formats:**
- ✅ Step-by-step processes
- ✅ Decision trees
- ✅ Cause-and-effect diagrams
- ✅ Timeline progressions
- ✅ Cyclical processes

---

### Information Graphics

**Purpose:** Communicate concepts or relationships

**Common formats:**
- ✅ Hierarchy diagrams (organizational, taxonomic)
- ✅ Comparison infographics
- ✅ Relationship maps
- ✅ Explainer diagrams
- ✅ System architecture visualizations

---

### Statistical Graphics

**Purpose:** Present statistics and proportions

**Common formats:**
- ✅ Percentage breakdowns
- ✅ Distribution visualizations
- ✅ Growth or decline curves
- ✅ Geographic/spatial data
- ✅ Statistical comparisons

---

## INFOGRAPHIC DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Visual Hierarchy

**Establish clear hierarchy:**
- ✅ Primary information most prominent
- ✅ Secondary information supporting
- ✅ Details accessible but not overwhelming
- ✅ Guide viewer eyes to key insights

**Techniques:**
- Size variation (larger = more important)
- Color emphasis (brand colors for primary)
- Positioning (top-left, center for prominence)
- Typography contrast (bold for key data)

---

### Simplicity & Clarity

**Keep infographics simple:**
- ✅ Single, clear message or insight
- ✅ No unnecessary decoration
- ✅ Clean, uncluttered layout
- ✅ Legible at final display size

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Overly complex data representations
- ❌ Too much information on one graphic
- ❌ Unclear relationships or connections
- ❌ Decorative elements that distract

---

### Consistency

**Design elements must be consistent:**
- ✅ Consistent icon style (if using icons)
- ✅ Consistent typography (Poppins only)
- ✅ Consistent color palette (brand colors)
- ✅ Consistent spacing and alignment
- ✅ Consistent visual language across series

---

### Data Integrity

**Represent data accurately:**
- ✅ True proportions in charts
- ✅ Accurate labels and legends
- ✅ Proper axis/scale representation
- ✅ No misleading visual tricks
- ✅ Source attribution when applicable

---

## INFOGRAPHIC COLOR PALETTE

Infographics use PRIMARY brand colors strategically:

**Primary colors:**
- SE Life Green (#3DCD58) — Primary data/emphasis
- SE Signature Green (#009530) — Secondary data
- SE Dark Green (#0A2F24) — Outlines, structure
- SE Light Green (#E7FFD9) — Background, fills
- SE Electric Green (#5EFF59) — Alerts, highlights
- White (#FFFFFF) — Reverse, contrast

**Color strategy:**
- Limit to 3-4 colors per infographic
- Use brand greens as primary palette
- Reserve white for contrast/separation
- One accent color maximum for emphasis

**❌ NOT APPROVED:** Secondary accent colors (Amber, Cyan, Blue, Red) are NOT standard in infographics (only if explicitly required for data differentiation).

---

## INFOGRAPHIC SIZING

### Standard Infographic Sizes

| Size | Dimensions | Use Case |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Full-Slide** | 10" × 5.625" (or portion) | Dominant visual, primary content |
| **Large** | 5.0" - 6.0" wide | Major infographic, significant content |
| **Medium** | 3.0" - 5.0" wide | Standard infographic, supporting |
| **Small** | 2.0" - 3.0" wide | Accent infographic, supplementary |

**Guidelines:**
- Maintain aspect ratio (don't distort)
- Ensure readability at final size
- All text, numbers, icons legible at size
- Test on projected display before finalizing

## TEXT IN INFOGRAPHICS

### Labels & Annotations

**Text elements in infographics:**
- ✅ Data labels (numbers, percentages)
- ✅ Category labels (x-axis, legend)
- ✅ Titles and headlines
- ✅ Annotations (arrows, callouts)
- ✅ Source attribution

**Guidelines:**
- Use Poppins font (only approved typeface)
- Size: 8pt - 14pt depending on hierarchy
- Color: Dark Green or White for contrast
- Align for clarity and professionalism

**Do NOT:**
- ❌ Use fonts other than Poppins
- ❌ Place text where it obscures data
- ❌ Use text so small it's illegible
- ❌ Inconsistent typography within graphic

---

## INFOGRAPHIC ACCESSIBILITY

### Data Accessibility

**Ensure data is accessible:**
- ✅ Clear legend or key
- ✅ Labeled axes and scales
- ✅ Data source cited
- ✅ Alternative text representation available
- ✅ Color not sole differentiator

**Example:** Don't rely on red vs green only; add labels, patterns, or numbers

### Alt Text for Infographics

**Provide descriptive alt text:**
- ✅ Describes main data/insight
- ✅ Key numbers or findings
- ✅ Type of visualization

**Example:**
- ✅ "Bar chart showing 45% renewable energy, 35% traditional, 20% alternative sources"
- ✅ "Process flow: five-step digital transformation journey from planning to implementation"

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Infographic" or "Chart"
- ❌ Overly long descriptions
- ❌ Skipping alt text

### Text Contrast

**If text overlays colored areas:**
- Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio (body text)
- Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio (large text)
- Test before finalizing
- Use semi-transparent background if needed

## INFOGRAPHIC BEST PRACTICES

### ✅ DO

- ✅ Start with clear insight or message
- ✅ Use appropriate visualization type for data
- ✅ Maintain visual consistency
- ✅ Test readability at final size
- ✅ Use brand color palette
- ✅ Label all data clearly
- ✅ Include source attribution
- ✅ Test accessibility and contrast

### ❌ DON'T

- ❌ Use unclear or misleading visualizations
- ❌ Distort data to make point
- ❌ Overcrowd with too much information
- ❌ Use decorative elements instead of clarity
- ❌ Forget to label axes, legend, units
- ❌ Use fonts other than Poppins
- ❌ Assume legibility without testing
- ❌ Mix visualization types without reason

---

## WHEN TO USE INFOGRAPHICS

### Use Infographics When:

- ✅ Presenting statistical data or numbers
- ✅ Explaining complex process or system
- ✅ Comparing multiple concepts
- ✅ Showing relationships or connections
- ✅ Making data memorable and engaging
- ✅ Guiding viewer through information
- ✅ Space limitations make text inefficient

### Avoid Infographics When:

- ❌ Data is too complex for single graphic
- ❌ Visualization type unclear or confusing
- ❌ Decorative elements outweigh information
- ❌ Simple text or table would be clearer
- ❌ Data integrity compromised by visualization
- ❌ Unable to test and verify accuracy
