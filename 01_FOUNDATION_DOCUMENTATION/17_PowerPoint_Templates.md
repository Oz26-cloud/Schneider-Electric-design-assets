## The Four Templates

| Template | File | Background | Logo (bottom right, locked) | Feel |
|----------|------|------------|-----------------------------|------|
| White | `AET_Template_white.pptx` | White `#FFFFFF` | SE Signature Green | Clean, minimal |
| Dark Green | `AET_Template_-_dark_green.pptx` | SE Dark Green `#0A2F24` | White | Premium, professional |
| Light Green | `AET_Template_-_light_green.pptx` | SE Light Green `#E7FFD9` | SE Signature Green | Fresh, modern |
| Gradient | `AET_Template_-_gradient.pptx` | One of the 4 approved gradients, on all slides | White | Dynamic, for large audiences |

Choose the template with the decision tree in `24_Template_Selection_Guide.md`.

---

## Rules That Apply to Every Template

- Use **one** template for the whole deck. Never mix templates.
- The logo is locked in the master slide. Never move it by hand.
- Use only the approved palette and Poppins. See Docs 05 and 06.
