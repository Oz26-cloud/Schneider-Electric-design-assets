# Brand Identity

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 1**

---

## OVERVIEW

Schneider Electric's brand identity is rooted in innovation, sustainability, and technological leadership. This document establishes the foundational brand principles that guide all visual communications.

---

## BRAND PURPOSE

To empower customers and partners through advanced energy management and automation solutions that drive sustainability and operational excellence.

---

## BRAND VALUES

- **Innovation:** Continuous advancement in technology and solutions
- **Sustainability:** Environmental responsibility and energy efficiency
- **Reliability:** Consistent, dependable performance
- **Collaboration:** Partnership and teamwork
- **Excellence:** High quality in all aspects

---

## BRAND PERSONALITY

Schneider Electric communications are:
- Professional yet approachable
- Forward-thinking and modern
- Trustworthy and grounded
- Clear and purposeful
- Energetic and dynamic

---

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

All brand materials use:
- **Primary color palette:** Greens (Life Green, Dark Green, Signature Green, Electric Green)
- **Typography:** Poppins font family (modern, clean, distinctive)
- **Imagery:** Professional, diverse, authentic
- **Shapes:** Geometric, flowing ovals and gradients
- **Style:** Flat design with organic elements

---

## PRESENTATION PRINCIPLES

### Clarity
Every communication has a single, clear message.

### Consistency
All materials follow the approved style system. For slides, see `02_WORKFLOW_LAYER/22_Slide_Setup.md`.

### Professionalism
Design choices maintain brand credibility and authority.

### Accessibility
All content meets WCAG contrast and readability standards.

### Innovation
Design feels contemporary and forward-looking while remaining timeless.
