# Tagline

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 18**

---

## TAGLINE OVERVIEW

Schneider Electric's official presentation tagline is:

**"Advancing Energy Tech"**

This tagline appears on select slides to reinforce brand messaging and positioning.

---

## TAGLINE USAGE

### When to Use
- Title slides
- Section dividers
- Closing slides
- Brand-forward moments

### When NOT to Use
- Every slide
- Technical content slides
- Data-heavy presentations
- Internal process slides

---

## TAGLINE PLACEMENT

**Standard position:** Bottom right of slide, above or integrated with logo area

**Minimum size:** 12pt Poppins Regular
**Recommended size:** 14-18pt Poppins Regular

---

## TAGLINE COLORS

- **On white/light backgrounds:** SE Signature Green or SE Dark Green
- **On dark backgrounds:** White or SE Electric Green
- **On gradients:** White (minimum 4.5:1 contrast required)
