# Schneider Electric Photography Framework

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 12**

---

## OVERVIEW

This document specifies photography guidelines, style, and usage for Schneider Electric PowerPoint presentations. Photography should reflect brand values, enhance storytelling, and maintain visual consistency.

**Core Principle:** Photography communicates brand personality, builds emotional connection, and reinforces key messages.

---

## PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE GUIDE

### Approved Photography Styles

**Schneider Electric photography emphasizes:**

- ✅ Real, authentic imagery (not overly staged)
- ✅ Professional quality and composition
- ✅ Technology and innovation focus
- ✅ People in work/professional contexts
- ✅ Industrial, energy, and sustainability themes
- ✅ Modern, forward-thinking aesthetic
- ✅ Diverse representation in people photography
- ✅ Clear, legible imagery (not overly artistic/abstract)

**Avoid:**

- ❌ Overly staged, artificial-looking photos
- ❌ Clichéd stock photography
- ❌ Blurry, low-resolution images
- ❌ Outdated visual style
- ❌ Extreme close-ups or abstract compositions
- ❌ Homogeneous representation in people photos

## PHOTOGRAPHY COMPOSITION

### Composition Best Practices

**Rule of thirds:**
- ✅ Place subjects off-center for visual interest
- ✅ Align horizon lines to thirds grid
- ✅ Create balanced, professional composition

**Depth and layers:**
- ✅ Include foreground, midground, background
- ✅ Creates visual dimension and interest
- ✅ Avoids flat, two-dimensional appearance

**Lighting:**
- ✅ Natural light preferred (authentic, flattering)
- ✅ Professional lighting when needed (high quality)
- ✅ Avoid harsh shadows or overexposure
- ✅ Consistent lighting across photo series

**Focus and blur:**
- ✅ Sharp focus on primary subject
- ✅ Appropriate background blur (depth of field)
- ✅ Clear subject emphasis
- ✅ Professional bokeh (if intentional)

## PHOTOGRAPHY COLOR & TONE

### Color Treatment

**Approved color approaches:**

**Option 1: Natural color (most common)**
- Authentic, unmanipulated colors
- Natural color grading and tone
- Professional editing only (no extreme color shifts)

**Option 2: Color grading (stylized)**
- Subtle color temperature adjustments
- Brand-aligned color grading
- Consistent grading across photo series

**Option 3: Black & white (selective use)**
- High-impact, timeless aesthetic
- Used for specific brand moments
- Clear, high-contrast composition

**Avoid:**

- ❌ Over-saturated, artificial colors
- ❌ Extreme color grading that obscures subjects
- ❌ Inconsistent color treatment across similar photos
- ❌ Washed-out, low-contrast images

### Tone and Mood

**Approved tones:**
- Professional, confident
- Forward-thinking, innovative
- Collaborative, inclusive
- Grounded, authentic
- Energetic, modern

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Corporate, sterile tone
- ❌ Overly casual or informal
- ❌ Pessimistic, dark mood
- ❌ Artificial, stock-photo feel

---

## PHOTOGRAPHY RESOLUTION & QUALITY

### Minimum Quality Standards

**Resolution:**
- **Minimum 1920 × 1080 pixels** for full-slide images
- **High-resolution preferred** (4K or higher for print-quality)
- **Maintain aspect ratio** when resizing

**File format:**
- **JPG** (compressed, standard web/presentation use)
- **PNG** (if transparency needed)
- **TIFF** (high-quality archival, rare use)

**Quality requirements:**
- ✅ Sharp focus on subject
- ✅ No visible compression artifacts
- ✅ Professional color grading
- ✅ No watermarks or logos (unless intentional brand element)

**Do NOT use:**

- ❌ Low-resolution, pixelated images
- ❌ Blurry or out-of-focus photos
- ❌ Heavily compressed/low-quality JPGs
- ❌ Watermarked stock photos (without license)

## PHOTOGRAPHY EDITING GUIDELINES

### Approved Editing

**Basic adjustments:**
- ✅ Exposure, brightness, contrast
- ✅ Color grading and temperature
- ✅ Cropping and straightening
- ✅ Sharpening and clarity
- ✅ Saturation and vibrance (subtle)

**Enhancement editing:**
- ✅ Spot removal (dust, minor imperfections)
- ✅ Vignetting (subtle, professional)
- ✅ Selective focus enhancement
- ✅ Professional color grading

**Do NOT:**

- ❌ Remove major elements (people, objects)
- ❌ Manipulate truth/mislead with editing
- ❌ Over-process images (obvious artificial look)
- ❌ Use heavy filters or extreme effects
- ❌ Add watermarks or text not part of original

### Image Sources

**Approved sources:**
- ✅ Original photography (Schneider Electric owned)
- ✅ Licensed stock photography (verified license)
- ✅ Professional photography commissions
- ✅ Employee/internal photography (with permission)

**Best practice:** Verify image licensing before use in presentations.

## PHOTOGRAPHY ACCESSIBILITY

### Alt Text for Images

Every photograph used in a presentation should have:
- ✅ Descriptive alt text (for screen readers)
- ✅ Clear, specific description
- ✅ Context within slide

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Team of engineers reviewing solar panel installation"
- ✅ "Woman technician adjusting industrial control system"
- ✅ "Manufacturing facility with renewable energy infrastructure"

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Photo 1" or "Image 1"
- ❌ Overly brief descriptions
- ❌ Alt text that repeats other slide text

### Image Contrast

**If text overlays image:**
- Ensure 4.5:1 contrast minimum (body text)
- Ensure 3:1 contrast minimum (large text)
- Use semi-transparent overlay if needed
- Test on actual image before finalizing

## PHOTOGRAPHY DO'S AND DON'Ts

### ✅ DO

- ✅ Use professional, high-quality imagery
- ✅ Choose authentic, authentic-looking photos
- ✅ Maintain consistent style across presentation
- ✅ Scale images proportionally
- ✅ Test image quality and readability
- ✅ Include diverse representation
- ✅ Use appropriate subject matter
- ✅ Verify image licensing

### ❌ DON'T

- ❌ Use low-resolution, pixelated images
- ❌ Use overly staged, clichéd stock photos
- ❌ Mix inconsistent photography styles
- ❌ Distort images (change aspect ratio)
- ❌ Use unlicensed or copyrighted images
- ❌ Overlay illegible text without contrast testing
- ❌ Use watermarked images (without license)
- ❌ Place images with no purpose or context
