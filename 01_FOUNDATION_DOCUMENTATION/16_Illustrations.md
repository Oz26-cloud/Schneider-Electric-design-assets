# Schneider Electric Illustrations

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 16**

## OVERVIEW

This document specifies illustration design and usage for Schneider Electric PowerPoint presentations. Illustrations add personality, warmth, and visual interest while maintaining brand consistency and accessibility.

**Core Principle:** Illustrations humanize content, make concepts approachable, and create emotional connection through visual storytelling.

## ILLUSTRATION STYLE CHARACTERISTICS

### Visual Style

**Approved illustration style:**
- ✅ Flat design aesthetic (no depth or 3D effects)
- ✅ Organic, flowing shapes and forms
- ✅ Fluid movement and dynamic composition
- ✅ Simple, clean linework
- ✅ Flattened perspective
- ✅ Modern, contemporary visual language
- ✅ Consistent style across entire library

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Photorealistic rendering
- ❌ Heavy shadows or 3D effects
- ❌ Overly detailed or intricate linework
- ❌ Inconsistent style or mixing sources
- ❌ Outdated or retro aesthetic

---

## CHARACTER DESIGN

### Character Characteristics

**Approved character design:**
- ✅ Slightly exaggerated proportions (expressive, not cartoonish)
- ✅ Diverse representation (skin tones, ages, body types)
- ✅ Professional attire and contexts
- ✅ Inclusive gender representation
- ✅ Accessible, relatable appearance

**Critical requirement:**
- ❌ NO facial expressions (neutral faces, no smiling/frowning)
- Reason: Allows universal interpretation and accessibility
- Characters engage through pose and context, not emotion
- Neutral faces reduce cultural/regional interpretation differences

## ILLUSTRATION COLOR PALETTE

Illustrations use PRIMARY brand colors with intentional ratio:

**Color distribution in illustrations:**
- **70%:** Neutral base colors (white, light gray, Dark Green outlines)
- **20%:** SE Life Green (#3DCD58) and primary brand colors
- **10%:** Secondary emphasis or accent details

**Approved colors for illustrations:**
- SE Life Green (#3DCD58) — Primary accent color
- SE Signature Green (#009530) — Secondary accent
- SE Dark Green (#0A2F24) — Outlines, structure, details
- SE Light Green (#E7FFD9) — Light fills, backgrounds
- SE Electric Green (#5EFF59) — Emphasis, highlights
- White (#FFFFFF) — Base, fills, contrast
- Dark Green (#0A2F24) — Outlines, structure

**Color approach:**
- Majority white/neutral with accent green
- Minimal, strategic color application
- Professional, sophisticated appearance
- Brand colors reinforce brand presence

**❌ NOT APPROVED:** Secondary accent colors (Amber, Cyan, Blue, Red) are NOT used in standard illustrations (only if explicitly required for specific narrative).

## ILLUSTRATION SIZING

### Standard Illustration Sizes

| Size | Width | Height | Use Case |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Extra Large** | 2.5" - 3.5" | 2.0" - 3.0" | Full-slide focus, primary visual |
| **Large** | 2.0" - 2.5" | 1.5" - 2.0" | Major accent, supporting focus |
| **Medium** | 1.5" - 2.0" | 1.0" - 1.5" | Standard illustration, supporting |
| **Small** | 1.0" - 1.5" | 0.75" - 1.0" | Accent element, detail |

**Guidelines:**
- Scale proportionally (maintain aspect ratio)
- Ensure character and detail visibility at size
- Larger illustrations for main narrative
- Smaller illustrations for supporting elements

## ILLUSTRATION PLACEMENT

### Positioning on Slides

**Illustrations can be placed:**
- ✅ Beside text (supporting narrative)
- ✅ Above text (introduction or context)
- ✅ Center of slide (dominant visual)
- ✅ Full-bleed or extending into margins (if design requires)
- ✅ Corner or edge (decorative accent)

**Typical placements:**

| Placement | Text Position | Illustration Role |
|-----------|---------------|------------------|
| **Beside text** | Text flows beside illustration | Supporting, equal visual weight |
| **Above text** | Below illustration | Introduction, context-setting |
| **Center dominant** | Minimal text or overlay | Primary focus, narrative center |
| **Full-bleed** | Edge to edge | Immersive, high-impact visual |

## ILLUSTRATION WITH TEXT OVERLAY

### Text Over Illustrations

When placing text over illustrations:

**High contrast required:**
- Test text legibility on illustration background
- Use semi-transparent overlay if needed
- Ensure minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- Light text on dark areas, dark text on light areas

**Best practices:**
- ✅ Place text in clear area (not obscuring characters)
- ✅ Use semi-transparent background for guaranteed contrast
- ✅ Test before finalizing on actual background

**Do NOT:**
- ❌ Place text over character faces
- ❌ Assume contrast without testing
- ❌ Make text illegible in presentation

## ILLUSTRATION ACCESSIBILITY

### Character Representation

**Illustrations must reflect diversity:**
- ✅ Multiple skin tones across library
- ✅ Various ages and body types
- ✅ Different gender representations
- ✅ Inclusive professional contexts
- ✅ Accessible and respectful portrayal

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Homogeneous or stereotypical portrayal
- ❌ Token diversity (one per illustration)
- ❌ Offensive or disrespectful imagery
- ❌ Excluding any demographic group

### Alt Text for Illustrations

**Provide descriptive alt text:**
- ✅ Describes scene and characters
- ✅ Notes relevant context or action
- ✅ Clear, concise description

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Diverse team of engineers collaborating on renewable energy project"
- ✅ "Professional woman using digital device in sustainable workplace"
- ✅ "Multigenerational team celebrating successful project completion"

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Illustration" or "Image"
- ❌ Overly detailed or too brief
- ❌ Describing only obvious elements

### Text Contrast

**If text overlays illustration:**
- Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio (body text)
- Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio (large text)
- Test before finalizing
- Semi-transparent background if needed

## ILLUSTRATION WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

### Step 1: Identify Narrative Need

**What story does this slide tell?**
- Introduction or context?
- Explanation or education?
- Celebration or achievement?
- Diversity or inclusion moment?
- Emotional connection or humanization?

### Step 2: Select Appropriate Illustration

From 196-illustration library, find best match:
- Represents narrative or concept
- Appropriate character diversity
- Aligns with slide message
- Matches intended tone

### Step 3: Determine Placement

**Where will illustration sit on slide?**
- Beside text (supporting)
- Above text (introduction)
- Center dominant (primary visual)
- Full-bleed (immersive)
- Corner/edge (accent)

### Step 4: Size Illustration Appropriately

- Full-slide narrative: Extra Large (2.5" - 3.5" wide)
- Major accent: Large (2.0" - 2.5" wide)
- Supporting element: Medium (1.5" - 2.0" wide)
- Detail/accent: Small (1.0" - 1.5" wide)

### Step 5: Position on Slide

- Respect safe content area (typical)
- Can extend into margins if designed intentionally
- Maintain white space around illustration
- Test on projected display

### Step 6: Add Contextual Text

- Brief explanation or narrative
- Connect illustration to slide message
- Ensure text legible if overlaying illustration
- Maintain text hierarchy

### Step 7: Verify Accessibility

- ✅ Add descriptive alt text
- ✅ Check text contrast if overlaying (4.5:1 minimum)
- ✅ Verify illustration legibility at final size
- ✅ Review for diverse representation
- ✅ Test on projected display

## WHEN TO USE ILLUSTRATIONS

### Use Illustrations When:

- ✅ Humanizing technical or abstract content
- ✅ Setting emotional tone or narrative
- ✅ Demonstrating concept through scenario
- ✅ Celebrating achievements or diversity
- ✅ Making content more relatable
- ✅ Breaking up text-heavy content
- ✅ Reinforcing brand values (inclusion, innovation)

### Avoid Illustrations When:

- ❌ Illustration purpose unclear or disconnected
- ❌ Slide already visually complex
- ❌ Illustration contradicts message
- ❌ Illustration reduces diversity or is stereotypical
- ❌ Photography or infographic more appropriate
- ❌ Text alone more effective
