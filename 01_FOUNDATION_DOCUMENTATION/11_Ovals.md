# Schneider Electric Ovals

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 11**

---

## OVERVIEW

This document specifies all approved decorative oval shapes for Schneider Electric PowerPoint presentations. Ovals add visual interest, break up content, and guide viewer attention while maintaining design consistency.

**Core Principle:** Ovals enhance visual hierarchy without cluttering slides or reducing readability.

---

## OVAL LIBRARY

Schneider Electric has 12 approved decorative oval shapes.

**Asset location:** 04_ASSETS/11_Ovals/ (12 oval files)

All ovals are pre-designed with approved brand colors and can be inserted into any PowerPoint slide.

---

## OVAL COLOR PALETTE

Ovals use PRIMARY brand colors only:

- SE Life Green (#3DCD58)
- SE Dark Green (#0A2F24)
- SE Signature Green (#009530)
- SE Electric Green (#5EFF59)
- SE Light Green (#E7FFD9)
- White (#FFFFFF)

**❌ NOT APPROVED:** Secondary accent colors (Amber, Cyan, Blue, Red) are NOT used for ovals.

---

## OVAL USES

### 1. Background Accent

Ovals as subtle background design elements:
- Behind text blocks
- Under headings
- Visual frame for content
- Color wash for emphasis area

**Typical opacity:** 60-80% (semi-transparent)

**Color:** Lighter green tones (Life Green, Light Green, Electric Green)

---

### 2. Visual Separator

Ovals to separate content sections:
- Between slide sections
- Break between topics
- Visual transition
- Organizational structure

**Typical opacity:** 100% (solid)

**Color:** Signature Green or Dark Green

---

### 3. Emphasis Highlight

Ovals to draw attention to important content:
- Highlight key information
- Circle around specific text
- Focus viewer attention
- Call-to-action emphasis

**Typical opacity:** 80-100% (mostly opaque)

**Color:** Life Green or Electric Green (bright, attention-grabbing)

---

### 4. Decorative Element

Ovals purely for visual design:
- Corner accents
- Edge details
- Design polish
- Aesthetic balance

**Typical opacity:** 50-70% (subtle)

**Color:** Any approved oval color

---

## OVAL SIZING

### Standard Oval Sizes

| Size | Width | Height | Use Case |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Large** | 3.0" - 4.0" | 2.0" - 3.0" | Major background accent, section separator |
| **Medium** | 1.5" - 2.5" | 1.0" - 2.0" | Standard emphasis, accent elements |
| **Small** | 0.5" - 1.5" | 0.5" - 1.0" | Decorative detail, corner accent |
| **Extra Small** | 0.25" - 0.5" | 0.25" - 0.5" | Bullet point alternative, fine detail |

**Guidelines:**
- Scale ovals proportionally (maintain aspect ratio)
- Adapt size to slide content density
- Larger ovals for minimal text slides
- Smaller ovals for text-heavy content

## OVAL OPACITY & TRANSPARENCY

### Opacity Guidelines

| Opacity | Visual Effect | Use Case |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| **100%** | Solid, fully opaque | Strong emphasis, separators, visual structure |
| **80%** | Mostly solid with subtle transparency | Emphasis highlight, moderate visual weight |
| **60%** | Semi-transparent, balanced | Background accent, subtle design element |
| **40%** | Transparent, subtle | Decorative texture, very light background |

**Rule:** Maintain text readability — if text overlays oval, ensure contrast ratio minimum 4.5:1.

---

## OVAL WITH TEXT OVERLAY

When placing text over ovals:

**High contrast required:**
- White text on dark green oval (✅ contrast 15.2:1)
- Dark green text on light green/white oval (✅ contrast 8.2:1)
- Dark Green text on Life Green oval (✅ contrast 7.0:1)

**Before finalizing:**
- ✅ Test contrast ratio (minimum 4.5:1)
- ✅ Verify readability on actual background
- ✅ Test on projected display

## OVAL BEST PRACTICES

### ✅ DO

- ✅ Use ovals to enhance visual hierarchy
- ✅ Scale ovals proportionally
- ✅ Maintain adequate white space around content
- ✅ Test text readability over ovals
- ✅ Use consistent oval styling across presentation
- ✅ Keep ovals within approved color palette
- ✅ Adapt oval placement to slide layout

### ❌ DON'T

- ❌ Overlap multiple ovals without clear hierarchy
- ❌ Use ovals with unapproved colors (secondary palette)
- ❌ Make ovals so large they dominate slide content
- ❌ Place ovals in ways that reduce text readability
- ❌ Use ovals without intention (only decorative)
- ❌ Distort ovals (maintain aspect ratio)
- ❌ Layer ovals so densely slide becomes cluttered

## ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

### Contrast Ratios

**If text overlays oval, verify minimum contrast:**
- Body text: Minimum 4.5:1
- Large text (18pt+): Minimum 3:1

**Testing:**
- Use WebAIM Contrast Checker for specific colors
- Test actual text on actual oval background
- Verify legibility on projected display

### Color Blindness Considerations

Ovals should not rely solely on color for meaning:
- ✅ Use value (brightness) variation
- ✅ Combine with text or other visual indicators
- ✅ Test in grayscale to verify visibility

---

## OVAL VARIATIONS

### Orientation Options

Ovals can be:
- ✅ Horizontal (wide, shallow)
- ✅ Vertical (tall, narrow)
- ✅ Circular (equal width/height)
- ✅ Angled/rotated (visual interest)

**Guidelines:**
- Maintain consistency in oval orientation across slides
- Use rotation sparingly for specific emphasis
- Vertical ovals work well for accent lines
- Horizontal ovals good for background shapes

---

## WHEN TO USE OVALS

### Use Ovals When:

- ✅ Slide needs visual hierarchy enhancement
- ✅ Breaking up dense text areas
- ✅ Emphasizing key information
- ✅ Separating content sections
- ✅ Adding design polish and professionalism
- ✅ Guiding viewer attention to specific content

### Avoid Ovals When:

- ❌ Slide is already visually complex
- ❌ Multiple competing design elements present
- ❌ Text-heavy slide with ovals would reduce readability
- ❌ Oval placement unclear or purely decorative with no purpose
- ❌ Ovals would obscure critical content
