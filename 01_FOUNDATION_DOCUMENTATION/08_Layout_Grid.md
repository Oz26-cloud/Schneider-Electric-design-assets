# Layout Grid

**Phase 1A Foundation Layer — Document 8**

---

## SLIDE DIMENSIONS

- **Size:** 10.0" × 5.625" (16:9)
- **Safe content area:** 8.98" wide × 5.155" tall
- **Left/Right margins:** 0.51"
- **Top margin:** 0.29"
- **Bottom margin:** 0.18" (footer area)

---

## MARGIN SYSTEM

Margins define the safe space around all content, ensuring consistency and protecting against edge clipping on various displays.

**All text and non-image content must stay within the safe content area.**

**Images can extend into margins if design requires.**

---

## GRID SYSTEM

Layouts are based on a flexible 6-column grid to create structure and alignment across all slides.

- **Two column:** ~4.0-4.25" each, 0.5-0.75" gap
- **Three column:** ~2.75-3.0" each, 0.35-0.5" gap
- **Four column:** ~2.0-2.2" each, 0.25-0.35" gap
- **Single column:** Full 8.98" width

---

## HEADER & FOOTER AREAS

- **Header (top 0.29"):** Reserved design space (logo NOT in this area)
- **Footer (bottom 0.18"):** Copyright + page number (fixed, locked)
- **Logo:** Right bottom corner (standard slides) or middle-top (back cover)
