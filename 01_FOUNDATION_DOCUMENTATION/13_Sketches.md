## SKETCH STYLE CHARACTERISTICS

### Visual Characteristics

**Approved sketch style:**
- ✅ Hand-drawn appearance (clean, not sloppy)
- ✅ Single or minimal line weight
- ✅ Technical/mechanical subjects
- ✅ Simple, clear compositions
- ✅ Minimal detail (not photorealistic)
- ✅ Modern, contemporary aesthetic
- ✅ Accessible to non-technical viewers

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Overly detailed technical drawings
- ❌ Photorealistic renderings
- ❌ Chaotic, messy linework
- ❌ Outdated or retro styling
- ❌ Overly simplified (cartoonish)

## SKETCH COLOR PALETTE

Sketches use PRIMARY brand colors with intentional, minimal application:

**Approved colors for sketches:**
- SE Life Green (#3DCD58) — Primary accent
- SE Signature Green (#009530) — Secondary accent
- SE Dark Green (#0A2F24) — Outlines, dark elements
- SE Light Green (#E7FFD9) — Light fills, backgrounds
- SE Electric Green (#5EFF59) — Emphasis, highlights
- Dark Green — Linework (sometimes)

**Color approach:**
- Minimal color palette per sketch
- Typically 1-3 colors maximum
- Emphasis on line over color
- Brand colors used strategically

**❌ NOT APPROVED:** Secondary accent colors (Amber, Cyan, Blue, Red) are NOT used for sketches.

## SKETCH SIZING

### Standard Sketch Sizes

| Size | Width | Height | Use Case |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Large** | 3.0" - 4.0" | 2.5" - 3.5" | Major accent, primary visual element |
| **Medium** | 2.0" - 3.0" | 1.5" - 2.5" | Standard illustration, supporting element |
| **Small** | 1.0" - 1.5" | 0.75" - 1.5" | Accent element, inline illustration |
| **Extra Small** | 0.5" - 1.0" | 0.5" - 1.0" | Icon-sized, minimal detail |

**Guidelines:**
- Scale sketches proportionally (maintain aspect ratio)
- Ensure detail remains visible at final size
- Larger sketches for complex concepts
- Smaller sketches for supporting elements

## SKETCH WITH TEXT OVERLAY

### Text Over Sketches

When placing text over sketches:

**High contrast required:**
- Test text legibility on sketch background
- Use semi-transparent background if needed
- Ensure minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- Light text on dark elements, dark text on light areas

**Best practices:**
- ✅ Place text in clear area (not overlapping complex details)
- ✅ Use semi-transparent overlay for guaranteed contrast
- ✅ Test before finalizing

**Do NOT:**
- ❌ Place text over busy sketch areas
- ❌ Assume contrast without testing
- ❌ Make text illegible in presentation

## SKETCH ACCESSIBILITY

### Alt Text for Sketches

Every sketch used should have:
- ✅ Descriptive alt text (for screen readers)
- ✅ Clear description of concept
- ✅ Context within slide

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Sketch showing solar panel array connected to power distribution grid"
- ✅ "Diagram illustrating building automation system with connected devices"
- ✅ "Workflow showing data collection, analysis, and decision-making process"

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Sketch 1" or "Image"
- ❌ Overly brief or vague
- ❌ Alt text that repeats other slide text

### Visual Clarity

Sketches must be:
- ✅ Clear and legible at final size
- ✅ High-resolution, sharp lines
- ✅ Sufficient detail to convey concept
- ✅ Not so detailed it becomes unclear

## SKETCH COLOR APPLICATION

### Color in Sketches

**How colors are used in sketches:**

**Line color:**
- Dark Green (#0A2F24) typically
- Creates definition and clarity
- Professional, readable appearance

**Fill color:**
- Brand green (Life, Signature, Electric)
- Light Green for light areas
- Selective color emphasis

**Color strategy:**
- Minimal colors per sketch (1-3 maximum)
- Colors used intentionally for emphasis
- Hierarchy through color and line weight
- Professional, controlled appearance

**Do NOT:**
- ❌ Recolor sketches (use as designed)
- ❌ Add secondary palette colors
- ❌ Over-saturate with color

## WHEN TO USE SKETCHES

### Use Sketches When:

- ✅ Explaining complex technical concepts
- ✅ Making technical content more accessible
- ✅ Illustrating process or workflow
- ✅ Adding visual interest to text-heavy slide
- ✅ Introducing innovation or forward-thinking topic
- ✅ Supporting sustainability or environmental message
- ✅ Breaking monotony of photography/text-only slides

### Avoid Sketches When:

- ❌ Sketch doesn't clearly relate to content
- ❌ Slide is already visually complex
- ❌ Sketch would obscure critical content
- ❌ Using purely for decoration without purpose
- ❌ A photograph or data visualization is more appropriate
