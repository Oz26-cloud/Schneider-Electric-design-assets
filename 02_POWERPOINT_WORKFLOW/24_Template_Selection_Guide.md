# Template Selection Guide

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 24**

---

## OVERVIEW

Template selection is Step 3 in the 8-step workflow. Choose ONE template and use it throughout the entire presentation.

Never mix templates. Never change templates mid-deck.

---

## 4 TEMPLATE OPTIONS

### **TEMPLATE 1: WHITE BACKGROUND**

**File:** `AET_Template_white.pptx`

**Visual Profile:**
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Logo: SE Signature Green (right bottom corner, locked)
- Primary Text: SE Dark Green or SE Life Green
- Secondary Text: Dark Green (#0A2F24)

**Energy Level:** Neutral / Professional
**Tone:** Clean, minimal, approachable, corporate

**Best For:**
- Most presentations (default safe choice)
- Sales pitches (professional, trustworthy)
- Training/education (readable, clear)
- Corporate communications
- Content-heavy presentations
- Client presentations (when unsure, use this)

**Avoid If:**
- Audience expects premium/executive feel
- Presentation topic is about innovation or cutting-edge technology
- Very large audience (gradient template more visible from far away)

**Color Usage on White Template:**
- Headings: Dark Green (#0A2F24) or Life Green (#3DCD58)
- Body text: Dark Green
- Accents: Life Green, Signature Green
- CTAs: Life Green buttons
- Graphics: Use all approved colors

---

### **TEMPLATE 2: DARK GREEN BACKGROUND**

**File:** `AET_Template_-_dark_green.pptx`

**Visual Profile:**
- Background: SE Dark Green (#0A2F24)
- Logo: White (right bottom corner, locked)
- Primary Text: SE Light Green (#E7FFD9)
- Secondary Text: White
- Accents: SE Life Green

**Energy Level:** High / Premium
**Tone:** Sophisticated, executive, authoritative, premium

**Best For:**
- Executive presentations (C-level, board-level)
- Premium positioning (high-value solutions)
- High-stakes pitches (investment, partnership)
- Awards or recognition presentations
- Evening/event presentations
- When you want to stand out visually

**Avoid If:**
- Text-heavy (light green text on dark can tire eyes with too much content)
- Older audience (may find contrast challenging)
- Printed handouts (dark background doesn't print well)

**Color Usage on Dark Green Template:**
- Headings: Light Green (#E7FFD9) or White
- Body text: Light Green
- Accents: White, Life Green, Electric Green
- CTAs: White buttons on dark, or Electric Green for high-impact
- Graphics: Use approved colors, ensure contrast

**Pro Tip:** This template is bold. Use it confidently, not hesitantly.

---

### **TEMPLATE 3: LIGHT GREEN BACKGROUND**

**File:** `AET_Template_-_light_green.pptx`

**Visual Profile:**
- Background: SE Light Green (#E7FFD9)
- Logo: SE Signature Green (right bottom corner, locked)
- Primary Text: SE Dark Green (#0A2F24)
- Secondary Text: Dark Green (#0A2F24)
- Accents: SE Life Green, SE Signature Green

**Energy Level:** Medium / Fresh
**Tone:** Modern, approachable, optimistic, energetic

**Best For:**
- Sustainability/ESG presentations (fresh, environmental feel)
- Product launch presentations
- Marketing/brand-forward presentations
- Educational/training content (friendly tone)
- Younger audiences
- Internal team presentations (fresh energy)
- When you want to feel "modern" but not "dark"

**Avoid If:**
- Audience expects traditional/conservative feel
- Very dark imagery (can clash with light background)
- Large blocks of text (can be harder to read on light green)

**Color Usage on Light Green Template:**
- Headings: Dark Green or Signature Green
- Body text: Dark Green
- Accents: Life Green, Signature Green
- CTAs: Life Green buttons (strong contrast)
- Graphics: All approved colors work well

---

### **TEMPLATE 4: GRADIENT BACKGROUND**

**File:** `AET_Template_-_gradient.pptx`

**Visual Profile:**
- Background: One of 4 approved SE gradients (applies to ALL slides)
- Logo: White (right bottom corner, locked)
- Primary Text: White or Electric Green
- Accents: White, Electric Green, Light Green

**Gradient Options:**
1. Diagonal TL-BR: Life Green → Dark Green
2. Horizontal L-R: Life Green → Signature Green
3. Vertical T-B: Signature Green → Dark Green
4. Diagonal TR-BL: Life Green → Electric Green

**Energy Level:** Very High / Dynamic
**Tone:** Cutting-edge, innovative, energetic, bold

**Best For:**
- Innovation/technology presentations
- Startup pitches
- Transformation/change narratives
- Visually impressive presentations (large venue, high-stakes)
- When you need maximum visual impact
- Future-focused content
- Exciting announcements

**Avoid If:**
- Content is data-heavy (gradients can make text harder to read in dense layouts)
- Audience is conservative (gradients can feel "too much")
- Printed handouts (gradients don't print well)
- Audience expects minimalist design

**Color Usage on Gradient Template:**
- Headings: White or Electric Green
- Body text: White (primary), Electric Green (emphasis)
- Accents: Light Green, Electric Green
- CTAs: White buttons or Electric Green
- Graphics: Use white, Electric Green, Light Green primarily

**Pro Tip:** Gradients are powerful but demanding. Use them when you want to make a statement, not when playing it safe.

## TEMPLATE SELECTION BY PRESENTATION TYPE

### **SALES PITCH**
- **Best choice:** White template (professional, safe, trustworthy)
- **Alternative:** Dark Green (premium positioning)
- **Why:** Client needs to focus on solution, not design. White is least distracting.

### **EXECUTIVE PRESENTATION**
- **Best choice:** Dark Green template (authority, premium)
- **Alternative:** White (if very data-heavy)
- **Why:** Executive audience expects sophisticated, polished look.

### **TRAINING/EDUCATION**
- **Best choice:** White template (clarity, readability)
- **Alternative:** Light Green (friendly, approachable)
- **Why:** Content clarity matters most. White is most readable.

### **EVENT/KEYNOTE**
- **Best choice:** Gradient template (maximum visual impact)
- **Alternative:** Dark Green (premium)
- **Why:** Large venue, far-away audience. Gradient visible and impressive from distance.

### **SUSTAINABILITY/ESG INITIATIVE**
- **Best choice:** Light Green template (environmental feel)
- **Alternative:** Gradient (energetic approach to sustainability)
- **Why:** Light Green connects visually to sustainability message.

### **PRODUCT LAUNCH**
- **Best choice:** Gradient template (exciting, new)
- **Alternative:** White (if product is traditional/industrial)
- **Why:** Product launch needs excitement. Gradient delivers that.

### **INTERNAL STRATEGY**
- **Best choice:** White template (focus on content)
- **Alternative:** Dark Green (serious, authoritative)
- **Why:** Internal audience cares about message, not visual pizzazz.

### **AWARDS/RECOGNITION**
- **Best choice:** Dark Green template (premium)
- **Alternative:** Gradient (celebration mood)
- **Why:** Prestigious tone needed.

### **INVESTOR/FUNDING PITCH**
- **Best choice:** White or Dark Green template (professional, trustworthy)
- **Alternative:** Gradient (if tech/innovation focus)
- **Why:** Investors need to see competence and clarity. White safest.

### **PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL**
- **Best choice:** White template (collaborative, safe)
- **Alternative:** Dark Green (premium positioning)
- **Why:** Partnership needs to feel like equal footing. White is neutral.

## DECISION TREE

**Use this to choose template quickly:**

```
START: What's the presentation about?

1. Is it VERY FORMAL or VERY HIGH-STAKES?
   YES → Use DARK GREEN template
   NO → Continue

2. Is it INNOVATIVE, EXCITING, or FORWARD-FOCUSED?
   YES → Use GRADIENT template
   NO → Continue

3. Is it SUSTAINABILITY, ESG, or ENVIRONMENTAL?
   YES → Use LIGHT GREEN template
   NO → Continue

4. Is it SALES, TRAINING, or CONTENT-HEAVY?
   YES → Use WHITE template (default)
   NO → Use WHITE template (if unsure, always default to WHITE)
```

## TEMPLATE RULES (CRITICAL)

### ✅ DO

- Choose ONE template at the start (Step 3)
- Lock your choice before designing slides (Step 4)
- Use the same template for every slide
- Stick with the template's color scheme
- Let the template's design system guide colors

### ❌ DON'T

- Mix templates in same presentation (NEVER)
- Change template mid-deck
- Use different template for different slide sections
- Override the template's color scheme
- Add custom backgrounds (defeats template system)
- Use gradient template with heavy text (unreadable)
- Use dark green template with light imagery (bad contrast)

---

## IF YOU CHANGE YOUR MIND

**What if you choose White but then want Dark Green?**

**START OVER.** Don't try to change templates mid-deck.

Why? The entire color scheme, text colors, and visual hierarchy are built for one template. Switching means:
- Redoing all text colors
- Redesigning every slide
- Breaking consistency

**Better approach:** If unsure, always start with White. It's the safest. You can always redesign in Dark Green later if needed.
