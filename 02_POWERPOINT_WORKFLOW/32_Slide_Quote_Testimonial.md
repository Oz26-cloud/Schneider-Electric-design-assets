# Slide: Quote/Testimonial

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 32**

---

## OVERVIEW

Quote/Testimonial slides add credibility through third-party validation. They show real-world impact and client satisfaction.

**Purpose:** Build trust, provide social proof, add emotion to presentation.

---

## CONTENT STRUCTURE

### QUOTE/TESTIMONIAL
- **Format:** Direct quote in quotation marks
- **Length:** 1-2 sentences maximum (short and punchy)
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold, 14-19pt
- **Color:** Template-dependent (use primary or accent color)

### ATTRIBUTION
- **Name:** Client/person who provided testimonial
- **Title:** Their role or company
- **Font:** Poppins Regular, 6pt
- **Color:** Secondary or subdued color

### VISUAL ELEMENT (Optional)
- Photo of person (if available)
- Client logo (if case study)
- SE illustration or icon (if no photo available)
- Background color or subtle shape for emphasis

---

## BEST PRACTICES

### ✅ DO

- Use real quotes from real clients
- Choose quotes that directly support presentation message
- Keep quote short and memorable
- Include attribution (name, title, company)
- Use visual (photo or logo) to add authenticity
- Format with quotation marks for clarity

### ❌ DON'T

- Use generic or vague quotes
- Make up testimonials
- Use quote without attribution
- Overcrowd slide with multiple testimonials
- Use low-quality or unclear photo
- Mix unrelated testimonials

---

## LAYOUT OPTIONS

**Option 1: Quote with Attribution**
```
"This solution reduced our energy costs by 30%."
— John Smith, VP Operations, Acme Corp
```

**Option 2: Quote with Photo**
```
[Photo]    "Quote text here"
           — Name, Title, Company
```

**Option 3: Client Logo + Quote**
```
[Logo]  "Quote text" — Name, Title
```

---

## COMMON MISTAKES & FIXES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Quote is too long (3+ sentences) | Trim to 1-2 sentences |
| Quote doesn't support message | Choose quote that directly proves your point |
| No attribution | Always include name, title, company |
| Photo is low quality or unclear | Use high-quality image only |
| Quote feels generic | Use specific, measurable quote |
