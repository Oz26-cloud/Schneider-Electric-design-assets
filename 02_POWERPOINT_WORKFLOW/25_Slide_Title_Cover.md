# Slide: Title/Cover

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 25**

---

## OVERVIEW

The title/cover slide is the first impression. It establishes brand, tone, and credibility in 3 seconds.

**Purpose:** Hook the audience, set expectations, establish SE brand authority.

---

## LAYOUT STRUCTURE

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                         │
│    [MAIN HEADLINE]                                      │
│    (Poppins SemiBold, 22pt max)                        │
│                                                         │
│    [Subheadline / Context]                             │
│    (Poppins Regular, 14-18pt)                          │
│                                                         │
│    [Optional: Tagline, Client Logo, or Visual]         │
│                                                         │
│                              [Date or Client Name]     │
│                                                         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

### MAIN HEADLINE
- **Text:** Clear, bold statement of presentation topic
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold
- **Size:** 18-22pt (max 22pt)
- **Color:** Template-dependent (see Template Color Guide)
- **Length:** Max 12 words (shorter is better)

**Examples:**
- "Advancing Your Energy Efficiency"
- "Digital Transformation in Industrial Systems"
- "Sustainable Solutions for Tomorrow"

### SUBHEADLINE
- **Text:** Context or benefit statement
- **Font:** Poppins Regular
- **Size:** 14-18pt
- **Color:** Secondary color (darker or lighter depending on template)
- **Length:** Max 20 words
- **Optional:** Can be omitted if headline is self-sufficient

**Examples:**
- "How SE solutions drive cost savings and sustainability"
- "A strategy for 2026 and beyond"

### VISUAL ELEMENT (Optional)
- Logo (client or SE)
- Gradient overlay
- SE oval or shape
- Icon or illustration (small, in corner or behind text)
- Image (full-bleed background)

**Rule:** Visual should support headline, not compete with it.

### FOOTER ELEMENTS
- Presenter name, date, or client name
- Minimal text (one line)
- 8-10pt, light color

## DO'S & DON'TS

### ✅ DO

- Keep headline short and punchy (max 12 words)
- Use only template's approved colors
- Leave plenty of white space (don't crowd)
- Make sure text is readable from far away
- Use headline to answer: "What will I learn?"
- Center align text (conventional for title slide)

### ❌ DON'T

- Use more than 2 lines of text for headline
- Add multiple visuals (keep it simple)
- Use small fonts (everything legible from 20 feet)
- Overcomplicate the design
- Use color outside approved palette
- Put the logo or tagline as main focus

---

## COMMON MISTAKES & FIXES

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Headline is too long (20+ words) | Hard to read, no impact | Cut to 12 words, remove filler |
| Text is too small (14pt headline) | Can't read from audience distance | Minimum 18pt, better 20-22pt |
| Mixed colors (3+ colors on slide) | Looks chaotic, amateur | Use max 2 colors + white/neutral |
| Too much visual clutter | Distracts from headline | Use ONE visual element, keep simple |
| Logo oversized | Competes with message | Keep logo locked in template size |
| No white space | Feels cramped | Leave 1-2" margins on all sides |
