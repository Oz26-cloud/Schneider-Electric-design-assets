# Slide: Image Showcase

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 30**

---

## OVERVIEW

Image showcase slides let visuals do the talking. They show the solution in action or demonstrate real-world application.

**Purpose:** Build emotional connection, show practicality, showcase product/solution in use.

---

## IMAGE SELECTION

### APPROVED SOURCES ONLY
- GitHub repo → 04_ASSETS/16_Illustrations/ (196 approved illustrations)
- SE official photography
- Client-provided brand images
- Professional stock (if SE-approved)

### REJECTED SOURCES
- Generic stock images (dated, clichéd)
- Competitor product images
- Low-resolution or watermarked images
- Images with brand confusion

### IMAGE REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum 1920×1080 resolution
- Professional quality
- Brand-compliant (no competitor products)
- Diverse representation (if people shown)
- No watermarks or logos

---

## LAYOUT OPTIONS

**Option 1: Full-Bleed Image**
- Image fills entire slide
- Headline as overlay (white text with contrast)
- Logo locked at bottom

**Option 2: Split Layout**
- 50% image, 50% text
- Image on left or right
- Headline + 2-3 bullets on opposite side

**Option 3: Image Grid**
- 3-4 related images in grid
- Small headline at top
- Each image ~3-4" wide

**Option 4: Image + Caption**
- Large image (4-5" wide)
- Headline + 1-2 sentence caption
- Clean white space around

---

## TEXT ON IMAGE

If text overlays image:
- **Contrast:** Minimum 4.5:1 (use white or dark green text)
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold or Regular
- **Size:** 14-19pt SemiBold (readable from far away)
- **Placement:** Top or bottom of image (not center-buried)
- **Background:** Subtle dark overlay behind text (optional, for contrast)

---

## BEST PRACTICES

### ✅ DO

- Use high-resolution images only
- Choose images that directly support message
- Use approved SE illustrations if photography unavailable
- Keep layout simple (one large image, not many small ones)
- Ensure text contrast meets 4.5:1 minimum
- Let image be the star (minimal text)

### ❌ DON'T

- Use low-quality or watermarked images
- Mix unrelated images on one slide
- Crowd slide with many small images
- Put text in center of image (hard to read)
- Use generic stock photos (dates presentation)
- Show competitor products or logos

---

## COMMON MISTAKES & FIXES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Image is low resolution/pixelated | Use 1920×1080 minimum, high-quality only |
| Text contrast is too low | Ensure 4.5:1 minimum, use white or dark text |
| Image has watermark | Remove or use different image |
| Multiple unrelated images | One strong image per slide |
| Text is hard to read on image | Add subtle dark overlay behind text |
