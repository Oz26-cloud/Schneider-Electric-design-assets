# Slide: Problem/Opportunity

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 27**

---

## OVERVIEW

Problem/Opportunity slides establish why the presentation matters. They answer: "Why should I care?"

**Purpose:** Create urgency or relevance, justify the need for solution, engage audience emotionally.

---

## CONTENT STRUCTURE

### HEADLINE
- **Text:** Problem statement or opportunity statement
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold, 18-20pt
- **Examples:** 
  - "Energy costs are rising faster than expected"
  - "Industrial productivity gaps are widening"
  - "Sustainability is now a competitive advantage"

### BODY TEXT
- **3-5 key points** supporting the problem/opportunity
- **Font:** Poppins Regular, 12-14pt
- **Format:** Bullet points (not full paragraphs)
- **Length:** 2-3 lines per bullet maximum

### VISUAL SUPPORT (Recommended)
- Chart/graph showing trend (declining efficiency, rising costs, etc.)
- Icon or illustration representing problem/opportunity
- SE oval or accent shape
- Limited imagery (supporting role, not focal point)

### LAYOUT
- Headline at top (centered or left-aligned)
- 3-5 bullets below (left-aligned)
- Visual to right side (optional)
- Maintain 0.4-0.5" spacing between bullets

---

## BEST PRACTICES

### ✅ DO

- Lead with headline that hits the core problem
- Support with 3-5 specific points
- Use data/statistics where possible
- Show trend (graph/chart) if numbers are key
- Create emotional connection (why it matters)
- Keep bullets short (max 2-3 lines each)

### ❌ DON'T

- Make it too negative or alarmist
- Include too much text
- Use vague statements ("Things are changing")
- Show too many statistics (limit to 2-3)
- Forget to tie problem to audience's world
- Crowd slide with text and visuals

---

## COMMON MISTAKES & FIXES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Too many bullet points (8+) | Limit to 3-5, combine related items |
| Problem is too vague | Make specific, quantifiable, relevant to audience |
| No supporting data | Add 1-2 statistics to strengthen claim |
| Text is too small (11pt) | Minimum 12pt, better 14pt |
| Visual competes with message | Use visual as supporting element only |
