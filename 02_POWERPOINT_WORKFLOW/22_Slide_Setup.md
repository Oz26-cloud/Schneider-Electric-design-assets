# Slide Setup

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 22**

These are the PowerPoint rules for slide size, margins, text and bullets. Use them for every deck. Brand specs (colors, font, logo) are in Docs 05–07.

---

## Slide Size and Areas

- **Slide size:** 10.0" × 5.625" (16:9)
- **Safe content area:** 8.98" wide × 5.155" tall
- **Left and right margins:** 0.51"
- **Top margin:** 0.29", reserved for template design (no content, no logo)
- **Bottom area:** 0.18", reserved for the footer (copyright and page number, locked)
- **Rule:** all text and non-image content stays inside the safe area. Images may extend into the margins.
- **Maximum line length:** 8.98"

---

## Grid

Layouts use a six-column grid.

| Layout | Column width | Gap |
|--------|--------------|-----|
| Two column | ~4.0–4.25" each | 0.5–0.75" |
| Three column | ~2.75–3.0" each | 0.35–0.5" |
| Four column | ~2.0–2.2" each | 0.25–0.35" |
| Single column | Full 8.98" | — |

## Text on Slides

| Level | Used for | Size | Weight | White template | Dark Green template | Light Green template | Gradient template |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 Headline | Slide title | 20–28pt | SemiBold | Signature Green `#009530` | Electric Green `#5EFF59`, Light Green `#E7FFD9` or White `#FFFFFF` | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | White `#FFFFFF`, or Electric Green `#5EFF59` for emphasis |
| H2 Heading | Section headers, subheadings, card headings | 14–19pt | SemiBold | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | Light Green `#E7FFD9` or White `#FFFFFF` | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Body | Body copy | 7–9pt | Regular | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | Light Green `#E7FFD9` or White `#FFFFFF` | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Small | Captions, footers | 6pt | Regular | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | Light Green `#E7FFD9` or White `#FFFFFF` | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Kicker | Label above a title | 6pt | SemiBold | Signature Green `#009530` | Electric Green `#5EFF59` | Dark Green `#0A2F24` | White `#FFFFFF` |

Rules that go with the table:
- Titles are always H1. Subheadings and card headings are always H2.
- Body copy is 7–9pt. Flag body text outside this range for review, and don't resize it without approval.
- Captions, footers and kickers are 6pt. Don't use 6pt for body copy.
- Text on a dark background uses Light Green, White or Electric Green, never Dark Green.

---

## Bullets

- Maximum 5 bullets per slide.
- Each bullet is 2–3 lines at most.
- Spacing between bullets: 0.4–0.5".

---

## Logo and Footer

- **Logo:** bottom right on standard slides, middle top on the back cover. It is locked by the template. Never resize or move it.
- **Footer:** the bottom 0.18" is reserved for copyright and page number.

---

## Slide Philosophy

- Each slide has a purpose
- White space is valued
- Hierarchy is clear
- Information is prioritized
- Visual interest is maintained through strategic color and imagery
