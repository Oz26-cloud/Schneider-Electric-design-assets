## LOGO

### LOGO PLACEMENT (FIXED IN MASTER SLIDE)

- **Standard slides:** Right bottom corner (locked, cannot be moved)
- **Back cover slide:** Middle of slide, towards top (locked)

## CTA

### CTA PLACEMENT BEST PRACTICES

#### Positioning

**Primary CTA:**
- Place in high-visibility area (center, lower-right, center-bottom)
- Never obscured by content
- Ample white space around button

**Secondary CTA:**
- Below or adjacent to primary CTA
- Slightly smaller or less prominent color
- Clear visual hierarchy

**Multiple CTAs:**
- Maximum 2-3 CTAs per slide (avoid decision paralysis)
- Primary CTA most prominent
- Secondary CTAs smaller or different color

#### Spacing

**Around buttons:**
- **Top/bottom:** 0.25" - 0.5" minimum white space
- **Left/right:** 0.25" - 0.5" minimum white space
- **Between multiple buttons:** 0.35" - 0.5" gap

**Text inside button:**
- See Button Padding section (0.1" - 0.2" vertical, 0.2" - 0.35" horizontal)

### CTA WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

#### Step 1: Choose CTA Type

- Button CTA → Standard, clickable, highly visible
- Text CTA → Subtle, inline, integrated with body copy
- Visual CTA → Directional, non-interactive, visual guidance

#### Step 2: Select Color

**Based on slide template and action importance:**
- Primary action on white slide → Life Green button
- Secondary action on white slide → Signature Green button
- Action on dark background → White button
- Text link → Dark Green underlined text

#### Step 3: Write CTA Text

- Action verb + clear outcome
- 2-4 words optimal ("Download PDF," "Schedule Demo")
- Sentence case or Title Case
- Never ALL CAPS

#### Step 4: Size & Position

- Use standard button size (Medium: 1.5" - 1.75" wide)
- Position in high-visibility area
- Maintain white space around button
- Add hover/active states (if interactive)

#### Step 5: Test

- Verify contrast ratio (minimum 4.5:1)
- Test keyboard navigation
- Check on projected screen (readability)
- Verify hyperlink functionality (if applicable)

### CTA EXAMPLES BY TEMPLATE

#### White Background Template

**Primary CTA:** Life Green button, white text, center-bottom placement
**Secondary CTA:** Signature Green button, white text, below primary

**Example:**
- Button 1: Life Green (#3DCD58), white text, "Explore solutions"
- Button 2: Signature Green (#009530), white text, "Contact us"

#### Dark Green Background Template

**Primary CTA:** White button, Dark Green text, center placement
**Text CTA:** Dark Green underlined link, integrated with body text

**Example:**
- Button: White background, Dark Green text (#0A2F24), "Request demo"
- Text link: "Learn more" in Dark Green with underline

#### Light Green Background Template

**Primary CTA:** Signature Green button, white text, lower-right placement
**Text CTA:** Dark Green link, subtle styling

**Example:**
- Button: Signature Green (#009530), white text, "Get started"
- Text link: "See pricing" in Dark Green

#### Gradient Background Template

**Primary CTA:** White button, Dark Green text, prominent placement
**Secondary CTA:** Life Green button, white text

**Example:**
- Button 1: White, Dark Green text, "Start now"
- Button 2: Life Green, white text, "Learn more"

## GRADIENTS

### GRADIENT TEMPLATE

**PowerPoint Template:** AET_Template_-_gradient.pptx

This template includes predefined gradients built into the master slide. Use this template when gradients are required.

### GRADIENT WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

#### Step 1: Decide if Gradient Needed

Is this a:
- ✅ Title slide? (use gradient)
- ✅ Section break? (consider gradient)
- ✅ High-impact moment? (use gradient)
- ❌ Dense content slide? (use solid background)
- ❌ Chart/data slide? (use solid background)

#### Step 2: Choose Gradient Type

| Message | Best Gradient |
|---------|---------------|
| **Energetic opening** | Gradient 1 (Life → Dark) |
| **Professional flow** | Gradient 2 (Life → Signature) |
| **Executive authority** | Gradient 3 (Signature → Dark) |
| **Innovation/cutting-edge** | Gradient 4 (Life → Electric) |

#### Step 3: Select Gradient Template

- Use AET_Template_-_gradient.pptx
- Template automatically applies chosen gradient to all slides
- Logo color and positioning already set

#### Step 4: Plan Text Content

- Minimal text on gradient (focus on clarity)
- Title + subtitle optimal; avoid body text on gradient
- Verify text color contrast (white or Dark Green)
- Test on projected display

#### Step 5: Verify Accessibility

- ✅ Test contrast ratio on actual gradient
- ✅ Ensure legibility in presentation room lighting
- ✅ Verify on multiple devices (laptop, projector)
- ✅ Test keyboard navigation (if interactive elements)

### WHEN TO USE EACH GRADIENT

#### Opening/Title Slide

**Best choice:** Gradient 1 (Life Green → Dark Green)
- **Reason:** Dynamic energy for first impression
- **Effect:** Bold, modern, brand-forward
- **Text:** White title and subtitle

**Alternative:** Gradient 2 (Life → Signature Green)
- **Reason:** Slightly more balanced, professional energy

---

#### Section Transition Slide

**Best choice:** Gradient 2 (Life Green → Signature Green)
- **Reason:** Visual separation with balanced flow
- **Effect:** Professional, organized presentation
- **Text:** White title, minimal supporting text

**Alternative:** Gradient 3 (Signature → Dark Green)
- **Reason:** Grounded, structured section break

---

#### Executive/Premium Moment

**Best choice:** Gradient 3 (Signature Green → Dark Green)
- **Reason:** Professional authority and confidence
- **Effect:** Premium, executive-level presence
- **Text:** White, concise messaging

**Alternative:** Gradient 1 (Life → Dark Green)
- **Reason:** Balance of energy with professionalism

---

#### Innovation/Technology Content

**Best choice:** Gradient 4 (Life Green → Electric Green)
- **Reason:** Cutting-edge, forward-thinking visual
- **Effect:** Modern, energetic, tech-focused
- **Text:** Dark Green or Signature Green (white may not have enough contrast)
- **Warning:** Use sparingly (very bright, can overwhelm)

## OVALS

### OVAL PLACEMENT

#### Positioning Guidelines

**Ovals can be placed:**
- ✅ Behind text (background layer)
- ✅ Beside text (accent element)
- ✅ Under headings (visual support)
- ✅ Corner placements (decorative)
- ✅ Full-bleed or edge-extending (if required)

**Margins:**
- Keep ovals within safe content area (typically)
- Can extend into margins or full-bleed if design requires
- Maintain white space around critical content

#### Background vs Foreground

**Background ovals (behind text):**
- Lower opacity (60-80%)
- Allows text to remain primary focus
- Subtle visual enhancement

**Foreground ovals (with text):**
- Text overlays oval
- Higher opacity (80-100%)
- Clear visual hierarchy (text readable)

### OVAL WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

#### Step 1: Determine Oval Purpose

What function does the oval serve?
- Background accent (enhance visual interest)
- Visual separator (divide sections)
- Emphasis highlight (draw attention)
- Decorative element (design polish)

#### Step 2: Select Oval Color

**Based on slide background and purpose:**

| Background | Recommended Oval Color |
|-----------|------------------------|
| White | Life Green, Signature Green, Dark Green |
| Dark Green | Life Green, Light Green, Electric Green |
| Light Green | Signature Green, Dark Green |
| Gradient | Life Green, Electric Green |

**Rule:** Ensure sufficient contrast between oval and background.

#### Step 3: Choose Oval Size

- Large ovals (3-4" wide): Major accents, minimal content slides
- Medium ovals (1.5-2.5" wide): Standard emphasis, moderate content
- Small ovals (0.5-1.5" wide): Decorative details, text-heavy slides
- Extra small ovals (0.25-0.5" wide): Fine details, subtle accents

#### Step 4: Position Oval

- Behind text: Lower opacity (60-80%)
- Beside text: Any opacity, no text overlap
- Under heading: Opacity 100%, supports visual hierarchy
- Corner/edge: Opacity 50-70%, decorative

#### Step 5: Adjust Opacity

- Verify text readability if oval behind/over text
- Test contrast ratio (minimum 4.5:1 if text overlays)
- Adjust opacity for visual balance
- Test on projected display

### OVAL USAGE EXAMPLES

#### Example 1: Background Accent on Title Slide

**Setup:**
- Large Life Green oval behind title
- Opacity: 70%
- White title text
- Adds visual interest without overwhelming

---

#### Example 2: Section Separator

**Setup:**
- Medium Signature Green oval between sections
- Opacity: 100%
- Visual break between content areas
- Helps organize presentation flow

---

#### Example 3: Emphasis Highlight

**Setup:**
- Medium Electric Green oval highlighting key statistic
- Opacity: 85%
- White or Dark Green text on oval
- Draws viewer attention to important point

---

#### Example 4: Corner Decorative Element

**Setup:**
- Small Light Green oval in bottom corner
- Opacity: 60%
- Purely decorative, adds design polish
- Does not interfere with content

## PHOTOGRAPHY

### PHOTOGRAPHY SIZING FOR POWERPOINT

#### Full-Width Image

**Dimensions:** 10" × variable height (maintains aspect ratio)
- Extends to slide edges (full bleed)
- Can overlap logo and footer (intentional design)
- Dominant visual element on slide

#### Half-Width Image

**Dimensions:** 4.5" - 5" × variable height
- Paired with text or another image
- Maintains safe content area margins
- Equal visual weight with text

#### Third-Width Image

**Dimensions:** 2.8" - 3.2" × variable height
- Three-column layouts
- Modular, repeating element
- Icons or supplementary imagery

#### Thumbnail Image

**Dimensions:** 1.5" - 2.0" × variable height
- Small accent images
- Icon-sized photos (rare)
- Detail or close-up element

**Rule:** Always scale images proportionally; never distort aspect ratio.

### PHOTOGRAPHY PLACEMENT

#### On Solid Backgrounds

**Standard placement:**
- Within safe content area
- Maintain margins from edges
- White space around images
- Text not overlapping image

**Extended placement (if required):**
- Can extend into margins
- Can overlap logo or footer if designed intentionally
- Full-bleed images for maximum impact

#### On Gradient Backgrounds

**Placement considerations:**
- Images can extend over gradient
- Images can overlap with gradient
- Test contrast and visibility on actual gradient
- Ensure image focal point remains clear

#### With Text Overlay

**When text overlays image:**
- ✅ Use semi-transparent overlay (if needed for contrast)
- ✅ Ensure text contrast minimum 4.5:1
- ✅ Place text on appropriate image area (dark or light)
- ✅ Test readability before finalizing

### PHOTOGRAPHY WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

#### Step 1: Identify Slide Purpose

What is this slide communicating?
- Introduction/context setting?
- Data/information visualization?
- Emotional connection/storytelling?
- Product/service showcase?
- Call-to-action moment?

#### Step 2: Select Image Subject

**Based on slide purpose, choose subject:**
- Technology/innovation imagery?
- People/collaboration photography?
- Sustainability/environmental focus?
- Business/operations context?

#### Step 3: Source High-Quality Image

- Minimum 1920 × 1080 pixels
- Professional quality (sharp, well-composed)
- Authentic, on-brand style
- Verified license/rights

#### Step 4: Size for PowerPoint

- Full-width (10" × aspect ratio maintained)
- Half-width (4.5" × aspect ratio maintained)
- Third-width (2.8-3.2" × aspect ratio maintained)
- Maintain aspect ratio, never distort

#### Step 5: Position on Slide

- Respect safe content area (typical)
- Can extend into margins if design requires
- Can overlap logo/footer if intentional
- Test final placement in context

#### Step 6: Verify Readability

- If text overlays image, test contrast (4.5:1 minimum)
- Ensure image focal point is clear
- Test on projected display
- Verify in actual slide context

### PHOTOGRAPHY IN CONTEXT

#### Title/Cover Slides

**Photography approach:**
- Full-bleed image across entire slide
- Strong, impactful visual
- Minimal text overlay (if any)
- Logo and footer still visible

**Image type:** Bold, attention-grabbing subject (technology, team, sustainability)

---

#### Content Slides

**Photography approach:**
- Half-width or third-width images
- Paired with text content
- Professional, supporting role
- Enhances narrative without dominating

**Image type:** Contextual, relevant to content (equipment, people working, process)

---

#### Data/Insight Slides

**Photography approach:**
- Smaller, accent-sized images
- Supports key insight or statistic
- Subtle background or emphasis element
- Hierarchy: data primary, image supporting

**Image type:** Relevant visual reference (industrial, technology, sustainability)

## SKETCHES

### SKETCH PLACEMENT

#### Positioning Guidelines

**Sketches can be placed:**
- ✅ Alongside text (margin space)
- ✅ Behind text (semi-transparent background)
- ✅ Center of slide (dominant visual)
- ✅ Corner or edge (accent element)
- ✅ Full-bleed or extending into margins (if required)

**Typical placements:**

| Placement | Context | Typical Size |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| **Beside text** | Text on left, sketch on right (or vice versa) | Medium |
| **Behind text** | Transparent sketch background, text overlaid | Large |
| **Center dominant** | Sketch as primary visual, minimal supporting text | Large |
| **Corner accent** | Sketch in corner, supporting main content | Small |
| **Full-bleed** | Sketch extends across entire slide | Large |

### SKETCH USAGE CONTEXTS

#### Title/Introductory Slides

**Purpose:** Set tone, introduce concept visually

**Sketch approach:**
- Large, prominent sketch
- Bold, attention-grabbing subject
- Minimal supporting text
- Logo and footer still visible

**Example subjects:** Power grid, renewable energy system, smart building concept

---

#### Concept Explanation Slides

**Purpose:** Illustrate technical concept accessibly

**Sketch approach:**
- Medium to large sketch
- Clear, simple composition
- Supporting text explains concept
- Sketch makes technical topic approachable

**Example subjects:** System flow, infrastructure diagram, technology process

---

#### Process/Journey Slides

**Purpose:** Show progression or workflow

**Sketch approach:**
- Process flow with arrows and steps
- Left-to-right or cyclical progression
- Each step clearly labeled or explained
- Sequential storytelling

**Example subjects:** Energy management workflow, digital transformation journey, supply chain flow

---

#### Supporting Content Slides

**Purpose:** Reinforce text with visual

**Sketch approach:**
- Smaller, accent-sized sketch
- Supports body copy
- Illustrates key concept
- Does not dominate slide

**Example subjects:** Collaboration concept, innovation symbol, sustainability principle

### SKETCH WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

#### Step 1: Identify Concept to Illustrate

What complex idea needs visual clarification?
- Technical system or infrastructure?
- Process or workflow?
- Innovation or technology concept?
- Sustainability or environmental topic?

#### Step 2: Select Appropriate Sketch

From 11-sketch library, choose best match:
- Matches concept or subject
- Appropriate visual complexity
- Aligns with slide message

#### Step 3: Determine Placement

**Where will sketch sit on slide?**
- Beside text (supporting)
- Behind text (background)
- Center dominant (primary visual)
- Corner accent (decorative)

#### Step 4: Size Sketch Appropriately

- Large slides with minimal text: Medium to large sketch
- Text-heavy slides: Small to medium sketch
- Accent role: Small sketch
- Maintain aspect ratio at all sizes

#### Step 5: Position on Slide

- Respect safe content area (typical)
- Can extend into margins if designed intentionally
- Maintain white space around sketch
- Test on projected display

#### Step 6: Add Contextual Text

- Brief explanation of concept
- Label key elements (if process diagram)
- Connect sketch to slide message
- Ensure text does not obscure sketch

#### Step 7: Verify Accessibility

- Add descriptive alt text
- If text overlays sketch, test contrast (4.5:1 minimum)
- Verify sketch legibility at final display size
- Test on projected screen

## ICONS

### ICON USAGE CONTEXTS

#### As Bullet Point Replacements

**Replace traditional bullets with icons:**
- ✅ Improves visual interest
- ✅ Aids content scannability
- ✅ Reinforces key concepts
- ✅ More engaging than text alone

**Guidelines:**
- Choose icon that represents concept
- All bullets in list use similar icon size
- Icons aligned consistently
- Text flows naturally from icon

---

#### As Visual Callouts

**Icons to highlight important information:**
- ✅ Feature highlights
- ✅ Key benefits
- ✅ Warning or alert
- ✅ Success or checkmark

**Guidelines:**
- Icon size medium to large
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Icon precedes or above text
- Label text concise and clear

---

#### In Process Flows

**Icons representing steps or stages:**
- ✅ Number or status progression
- ✅ Decision points or branches
- ✅ Input/output or transformation
- ✅ Start/end points

**Guidelines:**
- Consistent icon sizing across flow
- Arrows or connectors between steps
- Labels for each step or stage
- Left-to-right or top-to-bottom progression

---

#### As Data Visualization

**Icons representing data categories:**
- ✅ Chart legends
- ✅ Data grouping
- ✅ Category identification
- ✅ Metric labeling

**Guidelines:**
- Small to medium icon size
- Consistent styling with chart
- Legend clarity
- Color-coded if applicable

### ICON PLACEMENT

#### Positioning Guidelines

**Icons can be placed:**
- ✅ Beside text (left or right aligned)
- ✅ Above text (centered or left)
- ✅ Inline with text (inline sizing)
- ✅ Corner or edge (decorative accent)
- ✅ Full-slide pattern (background texture)

**Typical placements:**

| Placement | Text Position | Icon Size | Use Case |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|----------|
| **Left of text** | Flows right from icon | Small-Medium | Bullet list, features |
| **Above text** | Centered below icon | Medium-Large | Feature callout, step |
| **Inline** | Text wraps around | Small | Inline emphasis |
| **Corner** | Independent of text | Small | Decorative, accent |
| **Full pattern** | Over pattern | Extra Small | Subtle background |

### ICON WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

#### Step 1: Identify Content Needing Visual Support

What information benefits from visual representation?
- Bullet points or feature list?
- Process or workflow steps?
- Data categories or grouping?
- Alert, warning, or important callout?

#### Step 2: Select Appropriate Icon

From 571-icon library, find best match:
- Represents concept accurately
- Style matches presentation aesthetic
- Appropriate level of detail
- Color works with slide design

#### Step 3: Determine Placement

**Where will icon sit on slide?**
- Beside text (supporting)
- Above text (introducing)
- Inline (flowing with text)
- Corner/edge (accent)

#### Step 4: Size Icon Appropriately

- List bullets: Small-Medium (0.25" - 0.75")
- Feature callout: Medium-Large (0.75" - 1.5")
- Full-slide accent: Large (1.0" - 2.0")
- Inline: Small (0.125" - 0.25")

#### Step 5: Position on Slide

- Align with content
- Maintain consistent spacing
- Respect safe content area
- Test on projected display

#### Step 6: Choose Color

- Use icon as designed (default)
- OR recolor to match template
- Ensure sufficient contrast on background
- Maintain consistency across related icons

#### Step 7: Add Supporting Text

- Label icon if meaning unclear
- Concise, clear accompanying text
- Icon supports text, not vice versa
- Test readability together

### ICON USAGE EXAMPLES

#### Example 1: Feature List with Icons

**Scenario:** Highlighting three key benefits

**Icon approach:**
- Three icons (one per feature)
- Medium size (0.75" - 1.0")
- Left-aligned with text flowing right

**Scenario:** Four-step workflow or journey

**Icon approach:**
- Four sequential icons
- Medium size (0.75" - 1.0")

#### Example 3: Alert or Warning

**Scenario:** Important notice or attention-getter

#### Example 4: Data Categories

## INFOGRAPHICS

### INFOGRAPHIC PLACEMENT

#### Positioning on Slides

**Infographics can be placed:**
- ✅ Center of slide (dominant visual)
- ✅ Full-bleed (edge-to-edge)
- ✅ Beside text (supporting role)
- ✅ Top of slide (introduction)
- ✅ Bottom of slide (conclusion/summary)

**Typical placements:**

| Placement | Text Position | Infographic Role | Use Case |
|-----------|---------------|------------------|----------|
| **Center dominant** | Below or title only | Primary focus | Data-heavy slide |
| **Top** | Below infographic | Introduces concept | Title with visual |
| **Beside text** | Left or right | Supporting | Balanced layout |
| **Full-bleed** | Overlay or edge | Immersive visual | High-impact moment |

### INFOGRAPHIC WORKFLOW FOR POWERPOINT

#### Step 1: Identify Data or Insight

**What needs to be communicated?**
- Statistical data or numbers?
- Process or workflow?
- Concept or relationship?
- Comparison or contrast?

#### Step 2: Choose Visualization Type

**Select appropriate format:**
- Data → Chart, graph, or comparison
- Process → Flow diagram, timeline
- Concept → Hierarchy, system, explainer
- Status → Progress bar, gauge, matrix

#### Step 3: Plan Information Hierarchy

**What's most important?**
- Primary insight (most prominent)
- Supporting data (secondary)
- Details (accessible but not overwhelming)
- Source/context (bottom or legend)

#### Step 4: Design Layout

**Sketch infographic composition:**
- Establish visual hierarchy
- Plan typography and spacing
- Select color scheme
- Identify where icons/illustrations fit

#### Step 5: Build Infographic

- Create in appropriate tool (PowerPoint, design software)
- Use brand colors and Poppins font
- Maintain consistent spacing
- Test readability at final size

#### Step 6: Add Text & Labels

- Label all data (numbers, units, categories)
- Add title/headline
- Include legend if needed
- Add source attribution

#### Step 7: Verify & Test

- ✅ Check data accuracy
- ✅ Test text legibility
- ✅ Verify color contrast (4.5:1 minimum)
- ✅ Test on projected display
- ✅ Ensure accessibility
