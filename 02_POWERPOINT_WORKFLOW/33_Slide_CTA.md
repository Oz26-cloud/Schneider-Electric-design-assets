# Slide: Call to Action (CTA)

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 33**

---

## OVERVIEW

CTA slides tell the audience exactly what to do next. They move from information to action.

**Purpose:** Guide next steps, create commitment, provide clear action path.

---

## CONTENT STRUCTURE

### HEADLINE
- **Text:** Action-oriented statement
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold, 20-28pt
- **Examples:**
  - "Let's Start Your Transformation"
  - "Ready to Optimize?"
  - "Your Next Steps"

### CTA BUTTON(S)
- **Text:** Action verb + object (2-3 words max)
- **Examples:**
  - "Schedule Demo"
  - "Request Proposal"
  - "Learn More"
  - "Contact Us"
- **Style:** Use CTA specifications from 09_CTA.md
- **Maximum buttons:** 2-3 per slide

### SUPPORTING TEXT (Optional)
- **Description:** 1-2 sentences explaining why action matters
- **Font:** Poppins Regular, 7–9pt
- **Purpose:** Create sense of urgency or benefit

### CONTACT INFORMATION
- **Email:** contact@schneider-electric.com
- **Phone:** +1 (555) 123-4567
- **Website:** www.schneider-electric.com
- **Font:** Poppins Regular, 7–9pt

---

## CTA BUTTON SPECIFICATIONS

**See 09_CTA.md for full button specs**

**Primary CTA Button:**
- Background: Life Green (#3DCD58)
- Text: White or Dark Green
- Font: Poppins Regular, 7–9pt
- Shape: Capsule (rounded)
- Stroke: 1pt, 2mm radius

**Secondary CTA Button (if needed):**
- Background: Signature Green (#009530)
- Text: White
- Font: Poppins Regular, 7–9pt

---

## BEST PRACTICES

### ✅ DO

- Use clear action verbs ("Schedule", "Request", "Contact", "Learn")
- Make button text short and specific
- Include 1-2 buttons maximum (not cluttered)
- Provide clear contact methods
- Create sense of urgency (if appropriate)
- Make buttons highly visible
- Test button colors meet contrast requirements

### ❌ DON'T

- Use vague CTA text ("Click here", "More info")
- Crowd slide with too many buttons
- Bury contact information
- Use buttons outside approved CTA specs
- Mix button styles (stick to approved colors)
- Leave any ambiguity about next steps

## COMMON MISTAKES & FIXES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Vague CTA text ("Click here") | Use specific action ("Schedule Demo") |
| Too many buttons (4+) | Maximum 2-3 buttons |
| Button colors don't meet spec | Use approved CTA colors only |
| No contact info provided | Include email, phone, website |
| CTA text is too long | Maximum 2-3 words |
