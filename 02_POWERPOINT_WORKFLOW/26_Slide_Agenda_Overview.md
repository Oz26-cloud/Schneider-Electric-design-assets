# Slide: Agenda/Overview

**PowerPoint Workflow — Document 26**

---

## OVERVIEW

The agenda slide sets expectations. It tells the audience what they'll learn and how the presentation flows.

**Purpose:** Build confidence (audience knows what to expect), establish structure, create roadmap.

---

## LAYOUT STRUCTURE

**Option 1: List Format (Most Common)**

## CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

### HEADLINE
- **Text:** "Agenda", "Today's Topics", "What You'll Learn", or similar
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold, 20-28pt
- **Color:** Template-dependent primary color

### LIST ITEMS
- **Number of items:** 3-5 items (max 5)
- **Text per item:** 2-4 words only (max one line)
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold, 14-19pt
- **Color:** Template-dependent body text color
- **Format:** Numbered list (1, 2, 3, 4) or bullet points

**Examples:**
```
1. Current Challenges
2. Proposed Solutions  
3. Expected Outcomes
4. Timeline & Investment
```

### VISUAL INDICATORS (Optional)
- Small icons before each item (optional)
- Line or connector between items (optional)
- Subtle background shapes (optional)
- Use SE ovals or sketches if desired

**Rule:** Keep visual simple. Agenda should be scannable in 5 seconds.

## BY TEMPLATE

### WHITE TEMPLATE AGENDA

```
Headline: Dark Green or Life Green
List text: Dark Green
Numbers: Life Green (lighter touch)
Visual: SE oval (20% opacity, corner) or accent line
Spacing: 0.4" between items
```

### DARK GREEN TEMPLATE AGENDA

```
Headline: White or Light Green
List text: Light Green
Numbers: White
Visual: Subtle oval (10% opacity) or accent line
Spacing: 0.4" between items
```

### LIGHT GREEN TEMPLATE AGENDA

```
Headline: Dark Green or Signature Green
List text: Dark Green
Numbers: Signature Green
Visual: SE oval (20% opacity) or accent line
Spacing: 0.4" between items
```

### GRADIENT TEMPLATE AGENDA

```
Headline: White
List text: White
Numbers: Electric Green or White
Visual: Minimal (gradient background is enough)
Spacing: 0.5" between items (larger for readability on gradient)
```

## DO'S & DON'Ts

### ✅ DO

- Keep items short (2-4 words each)
- Number items (1, 2, 3, 4)
- Use parallel structure ("Topic", "Topic", "Topic")
- Leave plenty of spacing between items
- Make it scannable in 5 seconds
- Align items vertically (left-aligned)

### ❌ DON'T

- Write full sentences (too much text)
- Use more than 5 agenda items
- Mix numbered and bullet points
- Crowd items together
- Use colors outside approved palette
- Animate bullet points (keeps it simple)

---

## COMMON MISTAKES & FIXES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Agenda items are full sentences | Cut to 2-4 word phrases |
| 7+ agenda items listed | Maximum 5, combine related topics |
| Items have inconsistent formatting | Use parallel structure ("The X", "The Y") |
| Items are too cramped | Space them 0.4-0.5" apart |
| Too many visuals/icons | Use visual accent only, keep simple |
| Font too small (5pt) | Minimum 6pt, better 7-9pt |
